Checklist Pro for YouTrack — Privacy Policy

Overview

Checklist Pro for YouTrack operates within the customer's YouTrack environment and is not a hosted service.

Customer Data

The Developer does not intentionally collect or store customer checklist data on Developer-controlled servers. Checklist and template information remains within the customer's YouTrack environment.

The plugin does not intentionally transmit customer checklist data, issue data, project data, or other customer content to the Developer.

Use of Data

The Developer does not use customer checklist data for advertising, profiling, AI model training, or selling or sharing customer data.

JetBrains Marketplace

JetBrains Marketplace may process information needed for Marketplace, payment, and licensing operations under JetBrains' own terms and policies.

Customer Responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for securing and administering their YouTrack environment and for the information and integrations configured within that environment.

Changes

If the plugin's technical architecture changes in a way that introduces external services, telemetry, analytics, remote licensing, cloud storage, AI services, or other network communication, this policy should be reviewed and updated to accurately describe the resulting data flows.
